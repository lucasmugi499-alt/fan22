# GoalPlace Competition Operations

GoalPlace records fixture lifecycles separately from sporting truth. This language defines
when the platform may treat an absent result as an operational problem without inventing what
happened on the field.

## Language

**Governed Fixture**:
A fixture created under a bound capture policy, so its reporting obligation is explicit rather
than applied retroactively.
_Avoid_: Legacy fixture when referring to a current reporting obligation

**Postponed Fixture**:
A fixture still expected to be played at a later kickoff. It remains scheduled at the revised
time rather than becoming a terminal outcome.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, abandoned

**Cancelled Fixture**:
A fixture no longer expected to be completed, including a match called off or abandoned
without a result.
_Avoid_: Postponed

**Unreported Match**:
A governed fixture whose reporting grace period has passed, which is still expected to have a
result, and for which no field, league, bilateral, or official result source exists.
_Avoid_: Unverified result, disputed result
